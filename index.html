The science, simply科学，简单说

Tiny messengers, big conversations微小信使，深远对话

Exosomes are vesicles about 30 to 150 nanometres across. Cells release them to carry proteins, lipids and RNA to their neighbours, like sealed letters passed between cells. Regenerative aesthetics uses these messages to support the skin's own repair and renewal. 外泌体是细胞释放的微小囊泡，直径约 30 至 150 纳米，负责把蛋白质、脂质与 RNA 传递给邻近细胞，就像细胞之间的密封信件。再生美学正是借助这些讯息，支持肌肤自身的修复与更新。
