Privacy Policy

Liora Life Science Sdn Bhd · Effective Date: November 3, 2025

1. Purpose of This Policy

Liora Life Science Sdn Bhd (“Liora Life Science,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) values the trust you place in us when you share your personal data. This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, use, disclose, store, and safeguard your personal data in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA Malaysia) and, where applicable, international data protection standards.

This Policy applies to all individuals whose personal data is processed by us, including customers, patients, business partners, suppliers, employees, and visitors to our websites or premises.

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

Depending on your relationship with us, we may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity and demographic details: full name, NRIC/passport number, date of birth, gender, nationality.
  • Contact details: residential and mailing address, email address, telephone number.
  • Professional and business information: job title, employer details, qualifications, and professional licenses.
  • Financial and transactional information: bank account details, billing records, payment history.
  • Health and medical information: only where necessary for the provision of our products and services, subject to strict confidentiality safeguards.
  • Digital and technical information: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, login credentials, and usage data when you interact with our websites or digital platforms.
  • Other information: any additional data voluntarily provided to us in the course of business dealings, surveys, or communications.

3. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

We process personal data for legitimate business and operational purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Service delivery: to provide, administer, and improve our products, services, and solutions.
  • Customer and stakeholder engagement: to communicate with you regarding inquiries, updates, promotions, and service-related matters.
  • Contractual obligations: to process transactions, manage accounts, and fulfill contractual commitments.
  • Regulatory compliance: to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and reporting obligations.
  • Risk management and governance: for internal audits, due diligence, fraud prevention, and quality assurance.
  • Research and development: to support innovation, product development, and clinical or scientific studies, subject to ethical and legal safeguards.
  • Employment and HR purposes: for recruitment, payroll, benefits administration, and employee management.

4. Disclosure of Personal Data

We may disclose personal data to the following categories of recipients, on a need-to-know basis and subject to confidentiality obligations:

  • Subsidiaries, affiliates, and related companies within the Liora Life Science group.
  • Business partners, distributors, and service providers engaged to support our operations (e.g., IT, logistics, payment processors, marketing agencies).
  • Professional advisors, including auditors, lawyers, and consultants.
  • Regulatory authorities, government agencies, and law enforcement bodies, where disclosure is required by law.
  • Any third party to whom you have expressly consented to disclosure.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to unaffiliated third parties.

5. Data Security and Safeguards

We adopt a combination of technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • Secure servers, firewalls, and encryption protocols.
  • Access controls and authentication measures.
  • Regular audits, monitoring, and staff training on data protection.

While we strive to maintain the highest standards of security, no system is entirely immune to risks. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Data Retention

Personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law, regulation, or contractual obligation. Once retention is no longer necessary, data will be securely deleted, anonymized, or archived.

7. Your Rights Under PDPA

You have the following rights under the PDPA:

  • Access: to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: to request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated data.
  • Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw consent to processing, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.
  • Objection: to object to certain types of processing, such as direct marketing.
  • Deletion: to request deletion of personal data, where permissible under law.

Requests should be made in writing to the contact details provided below. We may require verification of your identity before processing such requests.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside Malaysia, we will ensure that the receiving jurisdiction provides a level of protection comparable to the PDPA, or that appropriate contractual and technical safeguards are in place.

9. Use of Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our websites may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to:

  • Enhance user experience and site functionality.
  • Analyze traffic patterns and usage trends.
  • Personalize content and marketing communications.

You may configure your browser to refuse cookies; however, certain features of our website may not function properly without them.

10. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, business practices, or technology. The updated version will be published on our website with the revised effective date.
